We've achieved 10-150x performance improvements while making the system easier to deploy and maintain than custom solutions."

### 3-Minute Deep Dive

"Most companies today have multiple AI models - recommendation engines, risk scorers, content modifiers. But these models don't coordinate. When they disagree, someone has to decide manually. When mistakes happen, there's no learning mechanism.

Aaroneous solves this by:

1. **Having specialists propose independently** - Each model suggests its solution
2. **Using consensus voting** - >66% agreement required for major decisions
3. **Learning from outcomes** - DNA Bank tracks what worked, improves proposals
4. **Scaling to multiple locations** - Multi-hive federation for geo-distributed systems
